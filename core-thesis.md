## 1. The Problem

Materialist frameworks assume that consciousness is an epiphenomenon — a byproduct of physical processes. Yet they cannot explain:

- Why subjective experience exists (the Hard Problem).
- Why we experience one outcome of a quantum event and not another (the Measurement Problem).
- Why time flows in one direction.
- Why memory persists despite physical brain damage.

ΨORM addresses these problems by reversing the foundational assumption: **consciousness is primary; matter is the interface.**

---

## 2. The Four Foundational Claims

### Claim 1: Time Is Not Fundamental
Reality is an infinite set of static "frozen moments." Time is not a dimension — it is the experience of consciousness illuminating these moments sequentially.

### Claim 2: Consciousness Is the Navigator
Consciousness is an active quantum waveform that selects and moves through frozen moments. It is not a passive observer — it is the driver of reality.

### Claim 3: Reality Is Frequency Synchronization
Consciousness interacts with the physical world through frequency entrainment and phase-locking with the biological receiver (the brain and body). The brain is a router; the soul is the cloud.

### Claim 4: Reality Is Not Shared
Each consciousness follows its own unique vector. Shared reality is an emergent property of frequency alignment, not an objective fact. Other beings are quantum shadows — projections of adjacent consciousness vectors.

## 5. Summary

ΨORM offers a unified, falsifiable framework that repositions consciousness as the primary agent of reality navigation. It provides a mechanism for:

- **Consciousness:** Frequency synchronization.
- **Reality navigation:** Attractor basin shifts.
- **Memory:** Cloud storage (the soul).
- **Shared experience:** Frequency alignment.
- **Anomalous phenomena:** Bleed-over, bleed-through, and duty cycle alteration.

The framework is open to refinement, critique, and experimental testing. It is a seed, not a cathedral — an invitation to explore, challenge, and extend.
