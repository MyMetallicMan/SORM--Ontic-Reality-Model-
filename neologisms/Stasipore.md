# Stasipore
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsteɪsɪpɔːr/
**Etymology:** Greek *stasis* (stable fixed point) + *poros* (passage, gate)

## Definition
A geospatial coordinate with high cross-basin stability (such as a road intersection) that acts as a preferred egress/ingress locus for vectors or ectophores moving between frozen moments.

## What it replaces
"Portal", "vortex", "haunted crossroads", "anomaly hot spot".

## Boundary Conditions
A stasipore is not a glowing magical gate. It is a coordinate whose features persist reliably across many frozen moments, lowering the energy threshold required for a frequency shift.

## ΨORM Cross-References
Vehicle ingress/egress, stable geospatial coordinates, frequency shift threshold

## Usage Example (Section XVII)
> Road intersections function as stasipores; their stable geometry lowers the threshold for ectophore vehicles to ingress or egress from the observer’s frozen moment.
