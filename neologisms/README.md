# SORM Neologisms
*Ψ-Ontic Reality Model (SORM) — Version 14.0*

## Purpose
This directory holds precision neologisms built exclusively for the internal mechanics of the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model.

Conventional English, standard physics vocabulary, religious terminology, and general philosophical language carry heavy pre-existing cultural and technical baggage. Words such as *world line, soul, heaven, field, memory* already have fixed outside definitions that drift away from the specific axioms of SORM. These neologisms do **not** replace the accessible, narrative language used across the main SORM document. Instead, they form a rigorous technical vocabulary for formal discussion, logical derivation, and precise notation within the model.

## Contents
Each term lives in its own standalone markdown file. Every entry includes:
- Pronunciation guide
- Etymology / compositional breakdown
- Literary pedigree (root history, prior usage notes)
- Exact SORM definition
- Where it supplements or replaces older, overloaded terminology
- Usage examples within SORM discourse
- Critical disambiguation: what this term **is not** (to block accidental mapping to mainstream physics or metaphysics)

## Ground Rule
> All terms herein are SORM-native. They cannot be assumed to align with relativity, quantum mechanics, theology, or standard ontology unless explicitly stated. They only gain meaning inside the axiomatic frame of SORM: reality is the frequency-basin environment surrounding a conscious experiencer; mind acts as a router rather than storage medium; garbons are grouped memory-quanta; swales are channels of energetic transfer between garbons.

## Naming Convention
Terms are compounded from classical Greek (primary) and Latin roots, selected to describe **function and relational role**, not visual metaphor or pre-existing cosmic mythology.
