# Synarmostasis
**Pronunciation:** /sɪnˌɑːrməˈsteɪsɪs/
**Etymology:** Greek *syn-* (together, with) + *harmos* (joint, fitting) + *stasis* (standing still, resistance).
**Related SORM Concepts:** Frequenbasin, Frequency Shift, Momentum, Affirmation Campaign
**Status:** Proposed Neologism (Contributed via peer-review)

## Core Definition (SORM Canonical Use)
Synarmostasis is the structural inertia or "friction" exerted by a current frequenbasin resisting a consciousness vector's shift to a new basin. It is the resistance that keeps a navigator trapped in old patterns. A sustained affirmation campaign wears down synarmostasis through the accumulation of frequency momentum, eventually allowing the vector to bypass the resistance and manifest the shift.

## Distinction Guardrails
- Not physical friction: It is an energetic/structural resistance in the configuration space.
- Not karma: Karma is dissonance *within* a soul; synarmostasis is the resistance *between* the soul and a basin.

## Literary & Technical Usage Examples
1. Doubling the campaign duration built enough momentum to break the synarmostasis of the old basin, collapsing a three-year timeline into eighteen months.
2. The practitioner failed to manifest because they did not generate enough momentum to overcome the local synarmostasis.

## Rationale for This Term
The phrase "friction" was intuitive but lacked precision. *Synarmostasis* accurately describes the structural resistance of a joined field (a basin) fighting against reconfiguration.
