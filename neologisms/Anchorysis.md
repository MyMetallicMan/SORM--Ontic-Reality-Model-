# Anchorysis
**Pronunciation:** /æŋˈkɒrɪsɪs/
**Etymology:** Greek *ankhura* (anchor) + *lysis* (loosening, release)

## Definition
An involuntary decoupling of a consciousness vector from its current frozen moment, followed immediately by re-anchoring into an adjacent frequency basin. This is the formal ΨORM name for the Emergency Slide / restart event.

## What it replaces
"Time reset", "I died and came back", "reality loop", casual near-death restart descriptions.

## Boundary Conditions
Anchorysis specifically denotes **re-anchored survival**. It is distinct from full decoupling without re-anchoring, which is the standard NDE transition into the cloud state.

## ΨORM Cross-References
Decoupling, Re-Anchoring, Emergency Slide, memory-garbon

## Usage Example (Section XVII)
> Anchorysis is triggered by extreme trauma; the vector detaches from the fatal frozen moment and locks into a neighboring basin where the harm never occurs. The memory-garbon of the fatal branch persists across the shift.
