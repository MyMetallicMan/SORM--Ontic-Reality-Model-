# Synnodosis
**Pronunciation:** /sɪˈnɒdəsɪs/
**Etymology:** Greek *syn* (together) + *hodos* (path / vector)

## Definition
Group vector coherence: two or more consciousness vectors enter bleed-through alignment and jointly illuminate the same alternate frozen moment.

## What it replaces
"Mass hallucination", "shared ghost sighting", "collective delusion", "group paranormal vision".

## Boundary Conditions
Synnodosis does not require every witness to perceive identical details. Their navigation vectors share the same bleed-through event, but individual sensory framing may still vary.
