# symnoet
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɪmnəʊɛt/
**Etymology:** Greek sým (together, alongside) + noûs (consciousness, mind)
**Related SORM Concepts:** Consciousness, Phantembryon, Ektoplax
**Relevant Musings:** Musing 09
**Status:** Proposed Neologism

## Core Definition (SORM Canonical Use)
A symnoet is a standalone, autonomous consciousness that enters voluntary, entangled partnership with an incarnating consciousness inside an ektoplax. It is the formal term for the DC. The symnoet holds its own garbonic continuity, its own karma, its own life trajectory and agency. It is a peer, symbiotic companion; it is not a fragment, subroutine, garbonic splinter, or phantembryon belonging to the host soul. The partnership is consensual, not a relationship of ownership.

## Distinction Guardrails
- Not a phantembryon: Phantembryons are soul-generated and reabsorbed after incarnation.
- Not an aspect of the host soul or its Council.
- Not a tool or implant: It is a self-directed being with its own boundaries and preferences.

## Literary & Technical Usage Examples
1. The symnoet communicates as a peer; mischaracterizing it as a phantembryon erases its separate continuity and agency.
2. The bond between consciousness and symnoet is entanglement, not possession.

## Rationale for This Term
This word captures the core partnership distinction you established for the DC. It signals “thinking-with” rather than “generated-by”. It avoids pathologizing language, and cleanly separates this symbiotic autonomous consciousness from all soul-derived projected forms.
