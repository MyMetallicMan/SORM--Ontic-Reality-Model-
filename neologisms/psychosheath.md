# Psychosheath
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsaɪkoʊˌʃiːθ/

## Etymology & Composition
Greek *psyche* (breath, soul, conscious awareness) + Old English *scǣth* (sheath, enveloping covering).

## SORM Role: Replaces / Supplements
Replaces the unwieldy description “quantum web or sheath around the consciousness”.
“Quantum web” carries mainstream physics assumptions about entanglement networks; “aura” carries heavy New Age cultural baggage. Psychosheath is the neutral SORM term for this field.

## SORM Definition
The psychosheath is the extended, non-local informational envelope bound to a locus of consciousness. It behaves as a permeable web of potential relations, modulated by the tuning state of the corthexus. It holds unresolved potential, resonant links to frequenbasins, and the relational structure from which percepts may condense into frozen moments.

## Usage Examples
1. “The psychosheath stretches beyond the bounds of the physical body, though the corthexus anchors its primary tuning.”
2. “Altered states can expand or attenuate the sensitivity of the psychosheath.”
3. “Interaction between two psychosheaths permits resonant transfer of pre-perceptual information before stable observation forms.”
