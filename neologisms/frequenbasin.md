# Frequenbasin
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfriːkwənˌbeɪsɪn/

## Etymology & Composition
Blend of Latin *frequentia* (frequency, recurrence) + *basinum* (basin, receptacle, bowl).
Literary pedigree draws from dynamical systems attractor basins, deliberately decoupled from standard deterministic chaos theory assumptions.

## SORM Role: Replaces / Supplements
Replaces the loose descriptive phrase "frequency basin".
The conventional "basin of attraction" carries baggage of deterministic state-space convergence. This neologism names a distinct SORM construct.

## SORM Definition
A frequenbasin is a bounded resonant domain that accumulates, filters, and stabilizes recurring informational harmonics. It is the containment envelope for a set of permissible phase relationships, not a physical container. Events or percepts that align with its harmonic profile tend to resolve within its bounds.

## Usage Examples
1. “The frequenbasin of this percept anchors the stable consistency of the frozen moment.”
2. “Consciousness may drift across frequenbasins when the dominant harmonic coupling weakens.”
3. “A frequenbasin is not a location in space; it is a regime of allowed resonance.”
