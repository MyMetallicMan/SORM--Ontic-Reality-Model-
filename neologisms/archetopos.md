# archetopos
## Pronunciation
/ɑːrˈkɛtəˌtɒpɒs/ (ar-KET-oh-top-os)

## Composition
Greek roots:
- *arkhē* (ἀρχή): origin, commencement, starting principle, seed
- *tópos* (τόπος): locus, situated point, localized place (not a planet or world)

## Literary Pedigree
*Arkhē* is a foundational term in pre-Socratic philosophy, used for the originating principle of a system. *Tópos* appears across rhetoric, geometry, and ontology to mean a “place of situatedness,” distinct from empty space. The compound itself is newly coined for SORM; this exact word has no established prior technical or literary definition.

## SORM Definition
A discrete, fixed experiential anchor point — the initial condition or seed-locus from which a consciousness’s frequency-basin reality may unfold. It is a static starting location, not a trajectory or path.

## Replacement / Supplement Note
Replaces the earlier informal label “frozen moment” and rejects the borrowed term “world line.”
A relativity world line describes the full continuous spacetime path of an object. An archetopos is only the anchor seed; it has no inherent path built into it. It precedes unfolding.

## Usage Examples
1. Every archetopos is a static experiential seed, waiting for the conscious router to initiate propagation outward through garbon networks.
2. One consciousness may hold multiple archetopoi across separate frequency basins.
3. A swale does not form inside an archetopos; it emerges in the flow departing from it.

## Disambiguation
- ❌ Not a coordinate in objective universal spacetime
- ❌ Not a timeline
- ❌ Not a snapshot of a pre-existing shared world
- ✅ A SORM-specific origin-locus for observer-tethered experiential reality
