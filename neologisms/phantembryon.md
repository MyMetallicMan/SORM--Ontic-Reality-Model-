# phantembryon
**Pronunciation:** /fænˈtɛmbriən/
**Etymology:** Greek phántasma (manifest appearance, projection) + émbryon (growth-seed, emergent formed entity)
**Related SORM Concepts:** Soul, Garbon, Council of Elders, Symnoet
**Relevant Musings:** Musing 09
**Status:** Proposed Neologism

## Core Definition (SORM Canonical Use)
A phantembryon is a sentient, manifest being woven from the originating soul’s garbons, dispatched into an ektoplax by the soul’s Council. Phantembryons serve specific learning purposes: companionship, instruction, friction/challenge, or support for the incarnating consciousness. While a phantembryon may develop unique personality, voice, and lived sentience during its time inside the ektoplax, it remains an aspect of its parent soul. At the end of the incarnation, it dissolves; its constituent garbons, memories and accumulated experience are reabsorbed back into the soul, and it does not become a separate, independent soul.

## Distinction Guardrails
- Emphatically not a symnoet (DC): A symnoet is a separate, autonomous consciousness with its own karma and trajectory.
- Not an independent soul.
- Not a passive illusion: It is real within the ektoplax, with genuine experiential weight.

## Literary & Technical Usage Examples
1. A phantembryon may speak with fierce authenticity and deep feeling, yet it remains tethered to the soul that seeded it.
2. The Council curates which phantembryons will accompany the consciousness before entry into the ektoplax.

## Rationale for This Term
This term formalizes the “projection” concept from Musing 09. “Phant-” acknowledges its projected origin, “embryon” emphasizes it is a living, growing seed-form for the duration of the incarnation. It avoids the weak connotations of “projection”, which many readers interpret as mere hallucination.
