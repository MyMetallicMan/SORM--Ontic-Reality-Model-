# Propaedeutic
**Pronunciation:** /ˌprəʊpɪˈdjuːtɪk/

## Etymology & Composition
Greek *propaideuein* — *pro-* (before, prior) + *paideuein* (to teach, train, educate).
Standard definition: introductory, foundational knowledge required before undertaking advanced study of a discipline. In SORM, this term is elevated from a description of textbook preparation to name a phase of pre-incarnate formation tied to stasichora and archetopos.

## SORM Role: Replaces / Supplements
Supplements the standard academic meaning.
In ordinary use, propaedeutic material is reading or instruction you consume *after* you are already present in the world. In SORM, propaedeutic describes the pre-birth formative training phase that occurs before a consciousness locus is injected into a prebirth world line template (the stasichora). This is not classroom learning; it is resonant attunement.
