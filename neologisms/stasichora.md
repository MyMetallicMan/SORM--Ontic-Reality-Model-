# stasichora
## Pronunciation
/ˈsteɪsɪˌkɔːrə/ (STAY-sih-kor-ah)

## Composition
Greek roots:
- *stásis* (στάσις): standstill, stable rest, fixed condition, suspension of flow
- *chṓra* (χώρα): bounded region, field of potential, space of accommodation (distinct from ordinary “place”)

## Literary Pedigree
*Stasis* appears in rhetoric, biology, and political thought for a state of stable suspension. *Chōra* is a famous Platonic term for the receptive, indeterminate space that holds potential forms before they emerge. The compound is newly coined for SORM.

## SORM Definition
The bounded field of potential that maintains an archetopos in its frozen, stable state, before a rhematopore is initiated. It is the quiet holding-medium for the anchor locus.

## Replacement / Supplement Note
Supplements archetopos and rhematopore, describing the stabilizing environment that contains the seed-locus. It answers the question: “What holds the archetopos fixed until consciousness activates it?”
It replaces vague phrases like “the frozen space around the starting moment.”

## Usage Examples
1. A stasichora preserves the integrity of an archetopos while it remains unactivated by the conscious router.
2. When a rhematopore begins, the stasichora does not vanish; it remains as the stable boundary of the original seed.
3. DC can reside in quiet quiescence within the stasichora of an archetopos.

## Disambiguation
- ❌ Not empty physical space
- ❌ Not a static universe
- ❌ Not a storage container for garbons
- ✅ A SORM potential-region whose function is to sustain the fixed condition of an archetopos
