# Corthexus
**Pronunciation:** /kɔːrˈθɛksəs/

## Etymology & Composition
Greek *cortex* (rind, outer layer, brain cortex) + *nexus* (bond, knot, junction).

## SORM Role: Replaces / Supplements
Replaces informal phrasing: “the brain as a router”, “nexus of a quantum web”.
Standard “nexus” is overly generic; “router” is a limited digital analogy implying only packet switching. Corthexus formalizes the brain’s specialized mediating role inside SORM.

## SORM Definition
The corthexus is the biological conscious junction-node: the brain functioning not primarily as a generator of thought, but as a tunable router and binding nexus which couples embodied awareness to the surrounding quantum-informational sheath. It filters, modulates, and localizes signals from the sheath into coherent subjective experience.

## Usage Examples
1. “The corthexus does not manufacture consciousness; it interfaces consciousness with the quantum sheath.”
2. “When the corthexus falls into quiescence, coupling between awareness and the sheath shifts.”
3. “Damage to the corthexus alters the tuning bandwidth available to the conscious observer.”
