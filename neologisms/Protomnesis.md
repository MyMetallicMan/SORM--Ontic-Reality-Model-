# Protomnesis
**Pronunciation:** /ˌprəʊtɒmˈniːsɪs/
**Etymology:** Greek *pro* (before / forward) + *mnesis* (memory)

## Definition
A memory-garbon retrieved from a temporally adjacent frozen moment that lies forward along the observer’s possible navigation branches. This is ΨORM’s precise term for what is commonly called premonition or prophetic dreaming.

## What it replaces
"Precognition", "premonition", "prophetic vision", "dreaming the future".

## Boundary Conditions
Critical semantic correction: protomnesis is **memory**, not prediction. The vector illuminates a forward frozen moment and carries the garbon backward; it does not calculate or forecast outcomes.

## ΨORM Cross-References
Non-physical cloud state, memory-garbon, temporal adjacency

## Usage Example (Section XVII)
> The dream was protomnesis, not prophecy; in low-filter non-physical state, the consciousness vector accessed a forward basin and returned bearing its memory-garbon.
