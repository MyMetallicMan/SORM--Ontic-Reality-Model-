# ektoplax
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɛktəplæks/
**Etymology:** Greek éktós (outside, external) + pláx (plane, configured surface, fixed field)
**Related SORM Concepts:** Toponarch, Garbon, Swale, Pre-Birth Template
**Relevant Musings:** Musing 07, Musing 09
**Status:** Proposed Neologism

## Core Definition (SORM Canonical Use)
An ektoplax is the external, shared, permanent learning template within configuration space. It is not generated, projected, or owned by any soul or soul-cluster. Souls enter the ektoplax, negotiate its conditions via their Council and the toponarch, and undergo experiential learning within its bounds. It persists across incarnations and remains intact when consciousness returns to non-physical states. An ektoplax may be physical, non-physical, or operate within alien dimensions inaccessible to human intuition.

## Distinction Guardrails
- Not a garbon: Garbons are quantum groupings of memory belonging to a soul.
- Not a swale: Swales are energetic connections between garbons.
- Not a projection / phantembryon: Phantembryons are soul-generated beings inside an ektoplax.
- Not a mental construct or internal soul projection: Its externality is the foundational axiom.

## Literary & Technical Usage Examples
1. Growth is possible within the ektoplax precisely because it lies outside the soul; the soul cannot defend or insulate itself from the template’s dissonance.
2. Our familiar physical reality is only one ektoplax among innumerable others in configuration space.

## Rationale for This Term
This term replaces the cumbersome phrase “pre-birth world-line template” in the revised Musing 09 framework. The root éktós embeds the critical correction: externality to the soul. It carries no prior New Age or philosophical baggage and refers strictly to the shared, fixed experiential field.
