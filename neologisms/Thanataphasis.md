# Thanataphasis
**Pronunciation:** /ˌθænəˈtæfəsɪs/
**Etymology:** Greek *thanatos* (death) + *aphasis* (utterance, signal)

## Definition
Bleed-through resonance between a living consciousness vector and the cloud-resident garbon-set of a vector that has fully decoupled from its biological receiver. This describes perceived communication with the deceased.

## What it replaces
"Talking to the dead", "mediumship contact", "spirit message".

## Boundary Conditions
Thanataphasis is resonance with stored garbons of a departed vector. It does not require the deceased vector to be actively, consciously “present” and speaking.
