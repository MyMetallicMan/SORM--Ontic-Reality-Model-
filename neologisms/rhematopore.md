# rhematopore
## Pronunciation
/ˈriːməˌtɔːr/ (REE-mah-tor)

## Composition
Greek roots:
- *rhéō* (ῥέω): to flow, stream, unfold, pour forth
- *póros* (πόρος): passage, channel, conduit, means of transit

## Literary Pedigree
*Rhéō* is the root of rhetoric and rheology; it carries the ancient association with continuous, directional flow. *Póros* means a passage or way across, used in natural philosophy for channels of movement. This compound is original to SORM and has no prior fixed usage.

## SORM Definition
The experiential propagation stream that unfolds outward from an archetopos, routed through connected garbons and mediated by swales of energy transfer. It is the living “path of becoming” extending from a static anchor.

## Replacement / Supplement Note
Supplements archetopos as its paired dynamic counterpart. It does not replace “world line” directly, but it takes the role people might mistakenly assign to world lines: the unfolding sequence after the starting locus.
Where an archetopos is the seed, a rhematopore is the flow.
