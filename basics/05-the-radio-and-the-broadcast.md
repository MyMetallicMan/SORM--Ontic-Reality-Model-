## The Mystery of Sleep

Every night, you go to sleep. 

You close your eyes. Your brain waves slow down. Your body becomes paralyzed so you don't act out your dreams. And then, you enter a strange, hallucinatory world where you can fly, where you meet the dead, where you see things that have not yet happened.

What is sleep? What is dreaming? What happens when you die?

To understand this, we need to understand how the brain works.

---

## The Brain is a Radio

Most scientists believe the brain is a generator. They believe it creates consciousness the way a generator creates electricity. When the generator breaks, the electricity stops. When the brain dies, consciousness stops.

SORM teaches that the brain is a **radio**.

Your consciousness is a broadcast signal. It exists independently of the radio. The brain is the receiver. It tunes into the signal and translates it into sound, vision, and thought. 

When you are awake, the radio is tuned to the physical station. You see the Garden. You hear the people around you. You feel the physical world.

When you sleep, the radio turns down the volume on the physical station. It begins to tune into other frequencies. This is what we call **dreaming**.

---

## The Two Frequencies

Your consciousness operates on two primary frequencies:

**The Physical Frequency (The Garden):**
This is the waking state. The radio is fully tuned to the body. You are interacting with the physical world. You are eating, working, talking, and playing. The filter is strong.

**The Non-Physical Frequency (The Home):**
This is the sleep state, the dream state, and the death state. The radio is tuned away from the body. You are interacting with the non-physical world. You are visiting your soul group. You are reviewing your life. The filter is weak.

---

## Why Dreams Matter

Dreams are not random brain noise. They are data.

When you sleep, your consciousness leaves the body and travels back to the Home. You visit your soul group. You review your lessons. You receive guidance from your Council. 

When you wake up, you bring back fragments of that experience. We call them dreams. Most of them are lost because the radio cannot perfectly translate the non-physical frequency into physical memory. But the ones you remember are messages. They are signposts. They are glimpses of the Home.

---

## The Radio and the Broadcast

Remember this: 

The broadcast is not the radio. The broadcast is independent. It exists forever.

The radio is temporary. It is a biological machine. It will eventually break. And when the radio breaks, the broadcast does not stop. It simply returns to the station.

You are the broadcast. You are not the radio.

This is why death is not the end. Death is just turning off the radio.
