# The Vehicle and the Driver

**SORM Basics**
**Author:** Robert Vannrox

---

## The Big Misunderstanding

Most people in the modern world believe a very specific story about who they are.

They believe that they are their brain. They believe that their thoughts, their memories, and their personality are generated by a lump of meat inside their skull. When the brain dies, they die. End of story.

This is the "Hard Drive" model. It treats you like a computer. 

SORM teaches something entirely different. It teaches the **Vehicle and the Driver** model.

---

## You Are the Driver

Imagine a car. 

The car has an engine, a steering wheel, and a radio. It is a highly complex machine. But the car is not the driver. The car is a vehicle that the driver uses to navigate the world. 

Your physical body—your brain, your organs, your senses—is the car.
Your consciousness—the real you—is the driver.

You do not live *in* your brain. You are connected *to* your brain. Your brain is a biological receiver. It takes the frequency of your consciousness and translates it into physical experience. It allows you to see, hear, touch, and taste. It allows you to interact with the Garden (physical reality).

But when the car breaks down—when the body dies—the driver does not die. The driver simply steps out of the vehicle.

---

## The Radio Broadcast

Think of it like a radio.

A radio signal is broadcast through the air. The radio in your living room picks up the signal and translates it into sound. If you smash the radio with a hammer, the sound stops. But did the broadcast stop? No. The signal is still in the air. You just destroyed the device that was translating it.

Your brain is the radio. Your consciousness is the broadcast.

When you sleep, the radio turns down its volume. When you dream, you are tuning into a different station. When you die, the radio turns off completely. But the broadcast—the real you—continues.

---

## What This Means for You

If you are not your body, then you do not need to fear the death of your body. 

If you are not your brain, then you do not need to fear the loss of your memories. Memory is just data stored in the cloud. You can access it again when you return Home.

You are not a machine. You are a traveler. You are a navigator. You are a conscious being temporarily using a biological vehicle to explore a physical world.

Drive the car. Do not let the car drive you. And never forget that you are not the vehicle. You are the one holding the steering wheel.
