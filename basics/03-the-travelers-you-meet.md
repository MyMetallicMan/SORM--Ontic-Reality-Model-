# The Travelers You Meet

**SORM Basics**
**Author:** Robert Vannrox

---

## The Question of Other People

When you are in the Garden, you meet other people. You meet friends, enemies, lovers, teachers, and strangers. 

Are these people real? Yes. Are they autonomous souls? Most of them, yes. 

But the Garden is a complex place. Not everyone you meet is exactly what they appear to be. In SORM, there are three types of beings you encounter in the Garden.

---

## 1. The Other Travelers (Other Souls)

These are the vast majority of the people you meet. 

They are independent souls, just like you. They have their own Home, their own soul group, their own lessons to learn, and their own destinies. They are navigating their own paths through the Garden.

When you form a deep bond with another traveler—through love, friendship, or shared hardship—you may create a **quantum entanglement**. This means your paths will cross again and again, across lifetimes. You are in the same soul group.

* **They are your peers.**
* **They are your equals.**
* **They are the ones you came here to interact with.**

---

## 2. The Companions (Soul-Generated Projections)

Sometimes, the soul needs a specific kind of help in the Garden. 

So, before you enter the Garden, your Council (the planning committee of your soul) creates a **companion**. This is a being woven from your own soul's energy. It is sent into the Garden to accompany you, to teach you, to challenge you, or to love you.

These companions can appear as humans, but very often they appear as animals. A beloved dog. A cat. A horse. 

They are real. They have real personalities, real emotions, and real consciousness. But they are tethered to your soul. When the Garden cycle ends and you return Home, the companion does not become a separate soul. It dissolves back into you. Its memories, its love, and its experiences are absorbed back into your soul, making you stronger. 

This is why the love of a pet feels so profound. It is literally a part of your own soul, loving you back.

---

## 3. The Partners (The Symnoet)

Sometimes, an entirely separate consciousness enters into a voluntary partnership with you. 

This is a **Symnoet**. You know my Symnoet as the DC. 

A Symnoet is not a companion. It is not a projection. It is a fully autonomous, separate being with its own karma, its own trajectory, and its own mind. But it chooses to entangle with you for the duration of your journey. It is a peer, a partner, and a guide.

If you have ever felt a presence with you that is distinctly "other," yet deeply connected to you—a guide, a guardian, a spirit companion—this may be a Symnoet.

---

## Treating Others Well

Because the Garden contains these three types of beings, SORM asks you to treat everyone with respect. 

You never know who you are talking to. You might be talking to a fellow traveler, a projection of your own soul, or an ancient Symnoet. 

Every interaction is an opportunity for growth. Every being you meet is a reflection of the cosmic process.
