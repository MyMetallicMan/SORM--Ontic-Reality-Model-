# The School Curriculum

**SORM Basics**
**Author:** Robert Vannrox

---

## Why Do Bad Things Happen?

The most common question people ask about any spiritual framework is this: *If the universe is good, why is there so much pain?*

Why do children get sick? Why do good people suffer? Why do we have to endure heartbreak, loss, and betrayal?

The materialist has no answer. They say the universe is random and indifferent. 

SORM has a different answer. SORM teaches that the Garden is a **School**.

---

## The Garden is a Classroom

Think of a child in a classroom. 

Does a good teacher give the child all the answers? No. A good teacher gives the child a problem to solve. The child struggles. The child makes mistakes. The child fails. And through that failure, the child learns.

If the teacher protected the child from every difficulty, the child would never grow. They would remain a baby forever.

The Garden is the classroom. The struggles, the pain, and the challenges are the curriculum. They are not punishments. They are **lessons**.

---

## What is Karma?

Most people think Karma is a cosmic system of punishment. If you do something bad, Karma will hurt you. If you do something good, Karma will reward you. It is like a giant cosmic ledger.

SORM teaches that Karma is not a ledger. **Karma is a tuning fork.**

Your soul is like a musical instrument. When you act in alignment with your deepest truth and love, your instrument is in tune. It resonates beautifully. This feels like "good karma."

When you act against your deepest truth—when you act in fear, in cruelty, or in ignorance—your instrument goes out of tune. It creates dissonance. It feels like "bad karma."

The purpose of the Garden is to learn how to tune your instrument. You are not being punished. You are being *corrected*. You are learning how to find harmony.

---

## The Cycle of Graduation

You do not stay in the classroom forever.

You enter the Garden. You learn your lessons. You experience pain and joy. You grow. You tune your instrument. 

When the cycle is over, you return Home. You rest. You review what you learned with your Council. And then, when you are ready, you come back to the Garden for a new grade level. A new set of lessons. 

You do this over and over again, across many lifetimes, until you have mastered the lessons. Until your soul is vast, coherent, and wise.

---

## Embracing the Struggle

If you understand that the Garden is a school, you stop asking, *"Why is this happening to me?"* and start asking, *"What is this teaching me?"*

The pain is real. The grief is real. SORM does not pretend that suffering is an illusion. But SORM reframes the suffering. It is not meaningless. It is the friction that polishes the stone. 

You are here to learn. You are here to grow. The curriculum is hard, but you are capable of graduating.
