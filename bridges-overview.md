## What Are These Bridges?

The Ψ-Ontic Reality Model (ΨORM) is a unified framework that repositions consciousness as the primary agent of reality navigation. It is a comprehensive model that draws on physics, neuroscience, philosophy, and the world's spiritual traditions.

However, not everyone comes to ΨORM from the same starting point. A physicist approaches reality differently than a Buddhist, and a Christian approaches it differently than a materialist.

These bridges are **accessible introductions** to ΨORM, tailored to specific worldviews. Each bridge uses the language, concepts, and values of its audience to present ΨORM as a complementary framework — not a replacement.

The goal is not to change anyone's beliefs. It is to invite exploration.

## On the Scope of These Bridges

These bridges cover a wide range of traditions and worldviews. They are not exhaustive. They are not authoritative interpretations of any tradition. They are invitations — doorways for the reader who already stands within a tradition and wants to see how ΨORM resonates with what they already know.

The bridges do not replace any tradition. They do not claim to speak for any tradition. They are written in the spirit of the real searcher — the one who is already asking deeper questions, who is not defensive about their background, and who will recognize truth when they see it.

Where a tradition is not represented, it is not because it lacks value. It is because a bridge must earn its place. A shallow bridge is worse than no bridge.
