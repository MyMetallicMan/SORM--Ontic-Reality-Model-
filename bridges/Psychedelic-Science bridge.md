## Introduction: A Shared Frontier

Psychedelic science is built on a foundation of profound discoveries:

- **The Default Mode Network (DMN):** The brain's "ego center," which regulates the sense of a separate self.
- **Ego-Dissolution:** The temporary loss of the boundary between self and world.
- **Neuroplasticity:** The brain's capacity to rewire itself in response to experience.
- **Mystical-Type Experiences:** States of unity, boundlessness, and profound meaning, reliably induced by psilocybin and other compounds.
- **The Entropic Brain Hypothesis:** The idea that psychedelics increase neural entropy, loosening the constraints of ordinary cognition.

These discoveries are not wrong. They are the leading edge of a revolution in consciousness research.

ΨORM does not reject these discoveries. It extends them. It offers a framework that deepens and illuminates the findings of psychedelic science. It provides a language and a structure for understanding *why* psychedelics work, *what* they reveal, and *how* they fit into a larger model of consciousness and reality.

This document is a bridge. It invites researchers, clinicians, and psychonauts to explore ΨORM without abandoning their empirical grounding. It shows how the core claims of ΨORM align with, deepen, and illuminate the findings of psychedelic science.

## The PiHKAL Archive: An Empirical Record of Reduced Filtering

Alexander Shulgin's *PiHKAL* and *TiHKAL* are systematic, phenomenologically detailed accounts of reduced-filtering states. Shulgin documented 179 phenethylamines and 55 tryptamines, including synthesis, dosage, duration, and detailed subjective effects commentary.

This archive is not a drug manual. It is an empirical record of what happens when the filters are loosened — recorded by a chemist who documented rather than preached. It is structurally credible in a way that mystical reports are not, because it makes no metaphysical claims. It simply reports what was experienced.

For ΨORM, PiHKAL and TiHKAL are a first-person archive of reduced-filtering states. They are a data source. They are not doctrine.

## What Psychedelics Are and Are Not

ΨORM does not claim that:

- Psychedelics reveal "the true reality." The filter is not a lie. It is a necessary interface.
- Psychedelic insights are inherently metaphysical truths. The content requires discernment.
- Every psychedelic experience is a valid navigation event. Reduced filtering makes the field more apparent, but it does not guarantee correct interpretation.

ΨORM does claim that:

- Psychedelics reduce filtering.
- Reduced filtering makes the frequency field more apparent.
- The experience is a rendering shift, not a hallucination.
- The insights are structurally real, even if their content requires discernment.
