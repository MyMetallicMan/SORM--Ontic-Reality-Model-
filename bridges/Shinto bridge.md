## Introduction: A Shared Way of the Kami

Shinto is built on a foundation of profound truths:

- **Kami:** The spirits or deities that inhabit all things — nature, ancestors, and sacred places.
- **Kannagara:** The way of the kami — the natural order, the flow of life, the way things are meant to be.
- **Musubi:** The creative power of becoming — the force that binds and generates life.
- **Misogi:** Purification — the practice of cleansing the self of impurity.
- **Harae:** Ritual purification — the removal of pollution and the restoration of harmony.
- **Makoto:** Sincerity — the quality of authentic, wholehearted action.
- **Kokoro:** Heart-mind — the seat of consciousness and feeling.
- **Shinbutsu-shūgō:** The historical synthesis of Shinto and Buddhism in Japan.
- **Matsuri:** Festival — the celebration of the kami through ritual and community.
- **Kami-dana:** The household shrine — the presence of the kami in daily life.

These truths are not wrong. They are the core of a tradition that has guided millions of souls for over two thousand years.

ΨORM does not reject these truths. It extends them. It offers a framework that deepens and illuminates the truths of Shinto. It provides a language and a structure for understanding the nature of consciousness, the kami, and the flow of reality that is consistent with the best of the Shinto tradition.

This document is a bridge. It invites practitioners of Shinto to explore ΨORM without abandoning their path. It shows how the core claims of ΨORM align with, deepen, and illuminate Shinto teachings.
