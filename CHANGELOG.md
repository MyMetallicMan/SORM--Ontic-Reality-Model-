# Release Notes — v14.0 (The Bridge Expansion)

**Date:** September 18, 2026
**Author:** Robert Vannrox

This release marks a major expansion of the SORM repository. The framework's ontology and mechanics are unchanged. What has changed is the accessibility of the framework to new readers.

## What's New

### Bridges: Expanded from 9 to 22

Thirteen new bridges have been added, covering:

- **Philosophical traditions:** Stoicism, Existentialism-Absurdism, Secular Humanism
- **Religious traditions:** Judaism, Sikhism, Jainism, Baha'i, Confucianism, Shinto
- **Esoteric traditions:** Thelema-Crowley, Golden Dawn, Witchcraft-Wicca
- **Scientific traditions:** Psychedelic Science

Each bridge presents ΨORM in the language and concepts of its audience. They are invitations, not replacements.

### New Section: XXXIII — The Experience of Consciousness at Death

A standalone synthesis of the ΨORM account of death as an eight-stage basin transition: decoupling, reversion, migration, arrival, integration, restoration, planning, and re-projection.

### Revisions to Existing Sections

- **Section VIII:** Added VIII.2.10 (Why Swales Are Necessary)
- **Section IX:** Added IX.3.1 (Swales at Death) and IX.3.2 (Reunification with Reserve Energy)
- **Section X:** Revised for the 4 Hz benchmark and state-dependent cycling
- **Section XVIII:** Added XVIII.3.1 (Pharmacological Reduction of Filtering)

### Repository Hygiene

- Removed the redundant `Bridges to Mainstream.md`
- Consolidated all bridge content into `bridges-overview.md`
- Updated README and MASTER-INDEX to version 13.7 / 14.0
- Expanded routing tables in README and MASTER-INDEX
