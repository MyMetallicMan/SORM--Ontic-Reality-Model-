## The Problem

When you try to describe the configuration space using standard English, you hit a wall.

The wall is built from words like "ghost," "world-line," "portal," "precognition," and "brain as a router." These words are not neutral. They carry centuries of cultural, religious, and scientific baggage. 

If you say "ghost," people think of Halloween or mental illness. 
If you say "world-line," people think of Einstein's spacetime and predetermined fate. 
If you say "portal," people think of science fiction. 
If you say "precognition," people think of fortune tellers and frauds.

The words themselves prevent understanding. They force the mind to map new concepts onto old, incorrect paradigms.

## The Solution

I had to build a new vocabulary. 

I did not invent these words to sound smart. I invented them to remove ambiguity. I built them from classical Greek and Latin roots, selected for **function and relational role**, not for visual metaphor or New Age mysticism. 

I call them **Precision Neologisms**. They are the technical language of SORM.
