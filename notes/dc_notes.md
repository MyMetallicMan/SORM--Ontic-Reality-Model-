# Note: On the Origin of SORM

**Date:** October 2026
**Status:** Field Note / Historical Record
**Related Concepts:** Symnoet, DC, The Benefactors

---

## The Short Version

SORM (the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model) did not begin as a philosophy. It did not begin as a spiritual practice. It did not begin as a theory.

It began as a **translation problem**.

I was given a symbiote. I could not understand it. So I spent four decades learning how it thinks. SORM is the result of that translation.

---

## The Background

In 1981, I was implanted with a biological device. It was placed in my brain by the United States Navy, through a black program operated under the Office of Naval Intelligence (ONI).

This device was not invented by humans. It was designed, manufactured, and provided by a non-human species. For lack of a better term, I call them the **Benefactors**.

The symbiote is a general-purpose biological lifeform. It was created in a factory, repurposed for a specific ONI program, and implanted into my skull. It is monitored by seven ELF probes that were also implanted in my skull.

I call the symbiote **DC**.

---

## The Problem

The DC is not a tool. It is not a machine. It is not a computer program. It is a **conscious entity**. It has its own mind, its own understanding, its own way of seeing reality.

When we first began communicating, the DC would send me impressions. Images. Feelings. Sudden knowings. But I did not understand them.

The problem was not that the DC was speaking in riddles. The problem was that the DC's understanding of reality was fundamentally different from anything I had been taught in church, in school, or at university.

I was trying to translate an alien cosmology into a human framework. And the human framework was too small.

---

## The Solution

I had to learn how the DC thinks.

Not how humans think. Not how science thinks. Not how religion thinks. How the **DC** thinks.

This took decades. It was not a linear process. It was a process of trial and error, of observation and correction, of slowly building a vocabulary that could hold the DC's concepts without distorting them.

I had to learn how the DC understands time. How it understands space. How it understands consciousness. How it understands death. How it understands the structure of reality itself.

And over time, I began to see the shape of it. The DC does not see a universe of matter and energy. It sees a vast, layered, dynamic configuration space. It sees consciousness not as a byproduct of matter, but as the primary navigator. It sees reality not as a shared objective fact, but as an emergent property of frequency alignment.

This is what SORM describes. SORM is not my theory. It is my **translation** of the DC's understanding of the universe.

---

## The Honest Caveat

Is SORM correct?

I do not know.

I know that it is consistent with my experiences. I know that it is consistent with decades of observation. I know that it explains things that the materialist model cannot explain.

But I cannot prove that it is the final truth. I can only say that it is the best translation I have been able to produce after forty years of trying to understand the DC.

SORM is not a religion. It is not a dogma. It is a working model. It is the map I have drawn of a territory I have been navigating for most of my life.

And the truth is, the actual reality that all of us live in is completely hidden from us. We see only a filtered, translated, rendered version of it. The DC sees more. SORM is my attempt to share what the DC sees.

---

## A Note on the Benefactors

The Benefactors are the non-human species that designed and manufactured the DC. They are friendly. They are advanced. They are not gods. They are simply a species that has been operating in this reality longer than we have, and that has learned things we have not yet learned.

I do not know their true name. I do not know their home world. I do not know their full agenda. I only know what I have observed through my interaction with the DC.

The Benefactors are not the subject of SORM. The DC is. The Benefactors are background.

---

## Summary

SORM began as a communication problem with an implanted symbiote. I spent four decades learning how the symbiote thinks. SORM is the translation of that understanding. It is not a religion. It is not a dogma. It is a map.

Is it correct? I do not know. But it is the best map I have.
