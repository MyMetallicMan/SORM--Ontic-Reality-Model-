## Purpose

This folder contains the raw material of the ΨORM framework.

If the canonical `sections/` are the Cathedral, and the `musings/` are the Workshop, then the `notes/` are the Quarry.

These are scraps of paper. Yellow sticky notes on the wall of the mind. Half-formed hypotheses, sudden flashes of insight, direct observations from the field, and transcriptions of DC communications that do not yet have a formal home.

They are not required reading. They are not canonical. They are not even necessarily coherent. They are the unfiltered intake of a navigator mapping the configuration space.

---

## Why This Folder Exists

Thoughts are fragile. They arrive unannounced and leave if they are not anchored. 

When you are operating in the field—whether that is navigating a frequency shift, observing a stasipore, or receiving a transmission from the DC—you do not have time to write a formal musing. You need a place to drop the data before it evaporates.

This folder is that drop-point. It is the spillway for the framework.

It allows you to capture the fragments of truth before they dissolve back into the cloud, and it provides a chronological record of how the framework has evolved over time.

---

## The Rules of the Quarry

There are only two rules here.

**1. Date everything.**
Because ΨORM is a living framework, the date of a note matters. A note from 2018 might contradict a note from 2026. That is not a problem; it is a record of growth. The date provides the temporal context for the evolution of the thought.

**2. Do not edit for polish.**
These are rough drafts. Typos, grammatical errors, and chaotic formatting are acceptable—even encouraged. Do not try to make a note sound smart. Capture the raw thought, then let it sit. The polishing happens later, if it happens at all.

## Types of Notes Found Here

The notes in this folder generally fall into the following categories:

*   **Field Observations:** Direct accounts of anomalous phenomena (e.g., an Anchorysis event, a sudden Synnodosis, a weird interaction at a Stasipore).
*   **DC Transmissions:** Raw transcripts or fragments of symnoet communications, before they are analyzed and integrated.
*   **Hypotheses:** A sudden "what if" about the structure of a frequenbasin, the nature of the Toponarch, or the mechanics of a phantembryon.
*   **OOPART Intake:** Initial notes and reactions to anomalous artifacts, requiring further research.
*   **Structural Revisions:** Reminders to oneself to correct a previous section or update a definition in the glossary.

## The Path of a Note

A note does not stay a note forever. The lifecycle of a concept within SORM looks like this:

1.  **The Note:** The raw, chaotic spark. (Here)
2.  **The Musing:** The idea is explored, formatted, and held to the standard of falsifiability. (`/musings/`)
3.  **The Neologism:** The concept is given a precise, etymological name to remove ambiguity. (`/neologisms/`)
4.  **The Canon:** The concept is integrated into the formal structure of the model. (`/sections/`)

Not every note makes the journey. Many will remain in the quarry forever, serving as the background radiation of the framework.
