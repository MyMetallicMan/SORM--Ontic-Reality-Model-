## The Contribution

While translating the SORM framework into Chinese, a follower named Tim caught a mechanical inconsistency in the text regarding the timing of manifestation. 

The original text stated that doubling the duration of an affirmation campaign "does not halve the manifestation time; it reduces it from 3 years to 1.5 years." This phrasing was mathematically contradictory.

Tim correctly identified that the relationship between campaign duration and manifestation time is non-linear, governed not by simple time math, but by **momentum** and **friction**.

## The Mechanics of Momentum

Tim's insight is now canonized in Section XII.6. 

1. **Momentum:** Every day an intention is held, structural momentum is built in the configuration space.
2. **Friction (Synarmostasis):** The old frequenbasin resists the shift. 
3. **Acceleration:** By extending the campaign, the practitioner wears down the friction of the old basin. Once the friction is reduced, the vector sinks deeper into the new basin, accelerating the manifestation. 

This is why a 3-year timeline can collapse to 1.5 years without violating the non-linear nature of reality.
