## Introduction

This glossary defines the key terms used throughout the ΨORM framework. It is intended to provide clear, consistent, and accessible definitions for all concepts, from foundational ontology to applied techniques.

Where possible, terms are defined with **operational definitions** — measurable quantities that can be observed and tested by independent researchers.

The glossary is organized alphabetically for easy reference.

---

## Glossary

### Attractor Basin
A stable region of the configuration space defined by a frequency attractor. Consciousness vectors within a basin are drawn to frequency-compatible frozen moments. Basins vary in depth, width, and stability.

**Related Terms:** Frequency Attractor, Configuration Space, Frozen Moment

---

### Biological Receiver
The physical body and brain. An access terminal to the quantum information repository (the soul). The brain is a router, not a hard drive. It renders reality by translating frequency input into sensory experience.

**Related Terms:** Router/Cloud Architecture, Soul, Consciousness Vector

---

### Bleed-Over
Incomplete decoherence between adjacent consciousness vectors. Occurs when frequency differences are small, resulting in partial overlap of experience. Bleed-over is the mechanism behind shared memories, synchronicities, and the Mandela Effect.

**Related Terms:** Bleed-Through, Quantum Shadow, Frequency Proximity

---

### Bleed-Through
Transient entanglement between previously orthogonal consciousness vectors. Occurs during rapid, extreme frequency shifts, allowing high-fidelity information transfer between vectors.

**Related Terms:** Bleed-Over, Quantum Shadow, Frequency Shift

---

### Configuration Space
The totality of all possible frozen moments. It is the high-dimensional space in which consciousness navigates. The configuration space is structured by frequency attractors and basins.

**Related Terms:** Frozen Moment, Frequency Attractor, Attractor Basin

---

### Consciousness
A projection of the soul into a specific frozen moment or sequence of frozen moments. It is the active, experiencing portion of the soul. Consciousness is the navigator — it selects, illuminates, and moves through frozen moments.

**Related Terms:** Soul, Consciousness Vector, Frozen Moment

---

### Consciousness Vector
The directed sequence of frozen moments illuminated by a given consciousness. It is the trajectory of experience. The sequential illumination of these frozen moments is the experience of time.

**Related Terms:** Consciousness, Frozen Moment, Time

---

### Duty Cycle
The proportion of time a consciousness spends in the non-physical state (the cloud) versus the physical state. A 50% duty cycle means equal time in both states. Altering the duty cycle affects access to the configuration space.

**Related Terms:** Non-Physical State, Physical State, Frequency Modulation

---

### Filter Dropping
**Operational Definition:** A reduction in alpha power (8–12 Hz) or an increase in broadband gamma power (30–100 Hz), indicating reduced sensory gating and increased access to the underlying frequency field.

**Measurement Protocol:** EEG: Measure alpha attenuation during meditation, altered states, or hypnagogic transitions.

**Related Terms:** Biological Receiver, Phase-Locking, Frequency Shift

---

### Frequency (Ω)
**Operational Definition:** The specific eigenvalue of the consciousness operator, corresponding to the dominant frequency band (e.g., alpha, beta, gamma) of neural oscillations.

**Measurement Protocol:** EEG spectral analysis: Identify the peak frequency band during intentional tasks.

**Related Terms:** Frequency Shift, Phase-Locking, Biological Receiver

---

### Frequency Attractor
**Operational Definition:** A stable PLV (Phase-Locking Value) pattern that emerges consistently across repeated trials or individuals.

**Measurement Protocol:** Cluster analysis: Identify PLV patterns that recur across trials.

**Related Terms:** Attractor Basin, Configuration Space, Frozen Moment

---

### Frequency Synchronization
The alignment of consciousness frequency with biological receiver frequency. This is the operational mechanism of reality navigation. When synchronized, the consciousness vector can navigate the configuration space with maximum efficiency.

**Related Terms:** Phase-Locking, Frequency Modulation, Consciousness Vector

---

### Frozen Moment
A static quantum configuration that constitutes one possible state of reality. Frozen moments do not change — they simply are. Consciousness navigates through them sequentially, creating the experience of time.

**Related Terms:** Configuration Space, Consciousness Vector, Time

---

### Garbon
An ordered quantum structure that forms naturally or is generated by consciousness. Garbons are the building blocks of the soul. Memory-garbons are passive records of experience; thought-garbons are active navigational modulators.

**Related Terms:** Quanta, Soul, Memory-Garbon, Thought-Garbon

---

### Group Vector
The collective trajectory of a cluster of frequency-compatible consciousness vectors. A group vector creates and occupies its own attractor basin, shaping the shared experiences of its members.

**Related Terms:** Consciousness Vector, Attractor Basin, Frequency Synchronization

---

### Mandela Effect
A phenomenon in which large groups of people share detailed, vivid memories of events, logos, quotes, or historical facts that do not align with the current frozen moment. In ΨORM, the Mandela Effect is explained as a collision of experiential histories resulting from group vector shifts.

**Related Terms:** Bleed-Over, Group Vector, Attractor Basin

---

### Memory-Garbon
A passive, past-oriented record of experience. Memory-garbons are stored in the soul (the cloud) and accessed by the biological receiver. They provide continuity and context for the consciousness vector.

**Related Terms:** Garbon, Thought-Garbon, Soul

---

### Non-Physical State
The state of consciousness when it is not phase-locked to a biological receiver. This includes sleep, dreaming, meditation, altered states, and the period between incarnations. The non-physical state allows direct access to the configuration space.

**Related Terms:** Duty Cycle, Physical State, Consciousness Vector

---

### Phase-Locking
**Operational Definition:** A state where the phase difference between two neural signals remains constant over time, with a Phase-Locking Value (PLV) approaching 1.

**Measurement Protocol:** EEG: Compute PLV between electrode pairs using Hilbert transform. PLV ranges from 0 (no locking) to 1 (perfect locking).

**Related Terms:** Frequency Synchronization, Biological Receiver, Consciousness Vector

---

### Physical State
The state of consciousness when it is phase-locked to a biological receiver. This is the ordinary waking state in which reality is rendered as physical experience.

**Related Terms:** Duty Cycle, Non-Physical State, Biological Receiver

---

### Quantum Shadow
A projection of another consciousness vector into a given consciousness's experiential trajectory. Quantum shadows appear to interact but are not actual co-occupants of a shared reality. They are the basis for the perception of other beings.

**Related Terms:** Bleed-Over, Bleed-Through, Consciousness Vector

---

### Quanta
The fundamental building blocks of reality. Quanta are the most basic units of the quantum information repository. They organize into garbons through natural entropic processes.

**Related Terms:** Garbon, Quantum Information Repository

---

### Quantum Information Repository
The non-local, non-temporal substrate in which all frozen moments and all memory states exist. It is the fundamental ontological ground of reality. It is the soul.

**Related Terms:** Soul, Frozen Moment, Garbon

---

### Router/Cloud Architecture
The model in which the brain is a router (access terminal) and the soul is the cloud (storage substrate). Memory is stored in the cloud, not in the brain. The brain accesses memory from the cloud and renders it into experience.

**Related Terms:** Biological Receiver, Soul, Memory-Garbon

---

### Soul
A complex, organized collection of garbons that has achieved sentience and agency through critical complexity and coherence. The soul is the quantum information repository (the cloud). It is eternal and persists across multiple consciousness projections.

**Related Terms:** Garbon, Consciousness, Quantum Information Repository

---

### Template Clustering
**Operational Definition:** The grouping of individuals by similarity of their Phase-Locking Value (PLV) patterns during shared intentional tasks.

**Measurement Protocol:** Hyperscanning EEG: Compute inter-subject PLV correlations and use hierarchical clustering to identify groups.

**Related Terms:** Group Vector, Frequency Attractor, Phase-Locking

---

### Thought-Garbon
An active, present-oriented navigational modulator. Thought-garbons are generated by consciousness through intention, attention, and agency. They modulate frequency and drive navigation.

**Related Terms:** Garbon, Memory-Garbon, Consciousness Vector

---

### Time
An emergent property of consciousness navigation. Time is not a fundamental dimension — it is the experience of sequentially illuminating frozen moments along the consciousness vector.

**Related Terms:** Frozen Moment, Consciousness Vector, Emergent Time

---

### Topography
The structure of the configuration space as a landscape of hills, valleys, peaks, plateaus, and canyons. The topography provides a visual and intuitive model for understanding navigation through the configuration space.

**Related Terms:** Configuration Space, Attractor Basin, Frequency Attractor
