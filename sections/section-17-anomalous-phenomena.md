## XVII.3 Categorization of Anomalous Phenomena

The reported phenomena can be grouped into several categories, each explained by specific ΨORM mechanics and precision terminology.

### Category A: Near-Death Experiences and "Restart" Events

#### A1: Anchorysis (The Emergency Slide)

**Reported Phenomena:**
- A person experiences a catastrophic event (car accident, fall, medical emergency) and "dies" — but then "restarts" moments earlier, unharmed.
- The person retains a vivid memory of the "death" event, including sensory details (sounds, smells, physical sensations).
- Often described as "I died but I didn't."

**ΨORM Explanation:**
This is **Anchorysis**: an involuntary decoupling of a consciousness vector from its current frozen moment, followed immediately by re-anchoring into an adjacent frequenbasin. 

During the catastrophic event, the biological receiver (the corthexus) cannot sustain the phase-lock. The vector detaches. However, instead of fully decoupling into the cloud state (which would result in biological death), the vector desperately seeks a stable resonance. It finds an adjacent frequenbasin — a frozen moment where the fatal harm did not occur — and locks in. The memory of the "death" moment persists across the shift as a memory-garbon.

This is not a hallucination. It is a real death in one frozen moment, followed by re-anchoring to a different frozen moment where survival occurred.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Decoupling** | The consciousness vector separates from the biological receiver. |
| **Re-Anchoring** | Instead of proceeding to the cloud, the vector re-anchors to a nearby frozen moment. |
| **Memory Transfer** | The memory of the "death" moment is carried over as a memory-garbon. |
| **Involuntary Nature** | The shift is not consciously controlled; it is triggered by extreme trauma. |

**Examples:**
- **Tractor-Trailer Accident:** A driver experiences a collision, dies, and then "restarts" moments before the impact, avoiding it.
- **Armoire Incident:** A person helping move furniture is crushed, then "restarts" at the dining room table.
- **Semi-Truck Near Miss:** A driver feels the impact of a collision, then "restarts" before the truck hits.

**The Ah-Hah Moment:**
> *"You did not imagine it. You did not hallucinate. You experienced a real death in one frozen moment — and then re-anchored to a different frozen moment where you survived. The memory is not false — it is from a different version of reality."*

### Category D: Protomnesis (Premonitions and Prophetic Dreams)

**Reported Phenomena:**

- A person has a "gut feeling" that something will happen, and it does.

**ΨORM Explanation:**

Critical semantic correction: Protomnesis is *memory*, not prediction. The vector does not calculate or forecast outcomes. It illuminates a forward frozen moment and carries the garbon backward.

**The Ah-Hah Moment:**
> *"You are not predicting the future — you are remembering a moment you have already illuminated in another part of the configuration space. Time is not linear; it is navigable. Precognition is memory of the future."*
