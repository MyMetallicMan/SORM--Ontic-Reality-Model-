## XXIX.1 Overview

One of the most significant features of the non-physical state is the periodic appearance of souls before a Council of Elders. This is not a courtroom of judgment — it is a gathering of wise, compassionate beings who assist the soul in reviewing past life performance, understanding karmic debts, and planning for future incarnations.

This section integrates Dr. Michael Newton's client-verified research into the ΨORM framework, presenting a coherent understanding of the Council of Elders and the karmic review process.

---

## XXIX.2 The Nature of the Council

### Composition

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Size** | 3–12 members, typically 6–8 |
| **Appearance** | Wise, robed beings, often appearing as elderly or androgynous |
| **Core Colors** | Purple, violet, deep blue — indicating high development |
| **Role** | Evaluators, guides, and planners for the soul's ongoing development |

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "The Elders are wise beings... a step or two above our guides... they are the most advanced identifiable entities my clients see." — *Destiny of Souls*

### The Chairperson

Each council has a chairperson who directs the proceedings and is the primary questioner. This Elder sits directly in front of the soul and usually wears a medallion with a symbolic design.

### The Presence

A higher energy force — simply called "the Presence" — is felt during council meetings. This is not God, but a high-level entity that assists the council in its work.

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "I feel the Presence as the ultimate energy. It is there for the council, but mostly for me." — *Destiny of Souls*

## XXIX.3 The Purpose of the Council

### Primary Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Life Review** | A comprehensive review of the soul's past life performance |
| **Karmic Evaluation** | Assessment of the soul's actions, choices, and consequences |
| **Lesson Planning** | Identification of lessons learned and those needing further work |
| **Future Planning** | Guidance for the selection of the next incarnation |
| **Accountability** | Souls are held accountable for their choices, but not punished |

**The Ah-Hah Moment:**
> "The Council does not judge — it guides. It does not punish — it plans. It does not condemn — it helps the soul understand."

### The Tone of the Meeting

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Compassion** | The Elders are loving and understanding |
| **Honesty** | No deception is possible; the soul knows its own truth |
| **Encouragement** | The Elders are supportive and constructive |
| **Accountability** | Souls are responsible for their own choices |

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "I tell the Council I could have done better. The Director says, 'We are not here to judge you, to punish you, or to override your thoughts. We want you to look at yourself through our eyes.'" — *Journey of Souls*

## XXIX.4 The Life Review Process

### Stages of Review

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **1. Overview** | The soul reviews major life events and decisions |
| **2. Key Choices** | Critical forks in the road are examined |
| **3. Relationships** | Interactions with others — especially soulmates |
| **4. Intentions** | The soul's motivations and intentions are examined |
| **5. Lessons Learned** | What was learned? What remains to be learned? |

### The Bus Stop Incident

A client in *Journey of Souls* was shown a small, seemingly insignificant act of kindness — comforting a woman at a bus stop — as one of the most important events of his life.

**The Ah-Hah Moment:**
> "No act of kindness is too small. The Council sees all — and values all."

## XXIX.5 Karmic Evaluation

### The Nature of Karma

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Not Punishment** | Karma is not punishment — it is balance |
| **Lesson Opportunity** | Karma provides opportunities for learning |
| **Justice and Mercy** | Karma is both just and merciful |
| **Self-Inflicted** | Souls are their own severest critics |

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "I serve as my most severe critic in front of the Elders. With the help of my counselors and peers, I decide on the proper mode of justice for my conduct." — *Journey of Souls*

### Karmic Payment

| Action | Karmic Consequence |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Harming others** | The soul may choose to experience similar harm in a future life |
| **Helping others** | The soul may be rewarded with better life circumstances |
| **Wasted opportunities** | The soul will face similar opportunities again |
| **Completed lessons** | The soul moves on to new challenges |

## XXIX.6 The Medallions of the Council

### Purpose

The medallions worn by some Elders are not symbols of rank — they are messages to the soul about their character and lessons.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Eagle** | Courage, rising above circumstances |
| **Grapes** | Growth, absorbing different aspects of life |
| **Key** | Unlocking knowledge |
| **Mountain Lion** | Courage in the face of danger |
| **Swirl** | Movement outward from the core |
| **Crooked Lines** | The imperfect path toward the goal |

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "The medallion is my lesson wheel. I have graduated to the emerald stone — the stone of the healer." — *Destiny of Souls*

### Gemstones on Medallions

| Gemstone | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Amber** | Nurturing, protection |
| **Emerald** | Healing |
| **Ruby** | Passion, intensity |
| **Sapphire** | Knowledge, truth |
| **Diamond** | Clarity, transformation |

## XXIX.7 The Law of Silence

Souls are encouraged to maintain privacy about their council meetings. This is not secrecy — it is respect for the sacred and personal nature of the evaluation.

**Client-Reported Experience:**
> "I don't want to break the Law of Silence... I don't want to share all my knowledge because it can create havoc if that person is not ready for certain things." — *Destiny of Souls*

---

## XXIX.8 The Second Council Meeting

Souls may appear before the Council a second time before rebirth. This meeting is more relaxed and focuses on future life planning.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Timing** | Just before the soul returns to Earth |
| **Tone** | More relaxed, forward-looking |
| **Focus** | Future life selection and planning |

## XXIX.9 Falsifiability

ΨORM predicts that:
1. Individuals in deep hypnosis will consistently describe the same general features of council meetings.
2. The experience of the Council will be perceived as compassionate and constructive, not punitive.
3. The symbols on medallions will have consistent meanings across clients.

If no such consistency is found, or if the experience is perceived as punitive, these predictions would be falsified.
