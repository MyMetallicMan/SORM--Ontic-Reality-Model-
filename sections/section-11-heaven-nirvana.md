## XI.4 Communities in the Non-Physical State

Consciousness vectors in the non-physical state can cluster into communities, just as they do in the physical state. These communities are formed by frequency compatibility and shared experiences.

**Implications:**
- The non-physical state is not isolation — it is a social environment.
- Consciousnesses can interact, learn, and grow in the non-physical state.
- The purpose of existence — soul growth — continues in the non-physical state.
