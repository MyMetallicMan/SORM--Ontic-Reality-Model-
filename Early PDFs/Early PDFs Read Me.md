## A Note to the Reader

These writings are not ΨORM. They are the archaeological record of how ΨORM was excavated.

Between 2017 and 2020, I was exploring ideas that would later coalesce into the Ψ-Ontic Reality Model. At the time, I was not writing a formal framework. I was thinking out loud — rambling, experimenting, and trying to make sense of a reality that did not fit the conventional narratives I had been taught.

These posts reflect that process. They are:

- **Raw** — Unedited, unfiltered, and often unfinished.
- **Influenced** — Shaped by my experiences, the American media, politics, and the cultural environment of the time.
- **Contradictory** — I hit many snags and dead ends. I went back and forth. That is how discovery works.
- **Personal** — These are not academic papers. They are the thoughts of a person trying to understand something that defied easy explanation.
