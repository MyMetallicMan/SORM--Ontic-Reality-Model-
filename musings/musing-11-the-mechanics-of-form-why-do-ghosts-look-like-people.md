## 1. Overview

We perceive ghosts as spirits with a defined (if transparent and changing) shape. Why the limits of size and shape? What is this spirit actually? 

If a ghost (a *Basinostate*) is just a consciousness vector trapped in inertia, why does it look like a person? Why does it wear clothes? Why does it have a defined, transparent, human-shaped boundary? Why doesn't it just look like a cloud of quantum static or a blob of glowing energy?

This musing proposes the SORM mechanism for the "Form of the Formless" and separates the phenomenon of the ghost from the biological anomaly of the poltergeist.

---

## 2. The Mechanism of Form: The Ghost

A ghost does not have a physical body. It does not have mass, DNA, or biological organs. So how does it cast a shape?

The answer lies in the interaction between the **Garbon**, the **Psychosheath**, and the living observer's **Corthexus**.

### 2.1 The Garbon Blueprint
A consciousness vector is not formless. It is tethered to its **garbons** (memory-quanta). When you live a physical life, your brain is constantly generating thought-garbons and memory-garbons encoded with sensory data. They hold the shape of your hands, the sound of your voice, the memory of the jacket you always wore. 

When the body dies, the vector separates from the flesh, but it does not immediately separate from the garbons. The garbons are the "blueprint" of the identity.

### 2.2 The Psychosheath as a Template
The **Psychosheath** is the extended informational envelope bound to the consciousness. In a normal migration, the psychosheath dissolves, and the garbons are reabsorbed into the cloud. 

But in a **Basinostate** (a stuck migration), the psychosheath is locked in *synarmostasis* (friction/inertia). It refuses to let go of the physical template. It stubbornly retains the energetic blueprint of the physical body it just lost.

### 2.3 The Living Brain as the Projector
A ghost does not project light; it projects information. 

When a living person (whose corthexus is tuned to the physical station) walks into a room with a Basinostate, their biological receiver picks up the "bleed-through" signal from the ghost's psychosheath. 

**The living person's brain is what gives the ghost its shape.**

Your brain expects a human spirit to look like a human. So when it receives the frequency imprint of the Basinostate's garbons, it renders a translucent human form, wearing the clothes from the memory-garbons. The ghost has form only because of the information it holds, and because the living observer's brain renders that information into visual shape.

### 2.4 The Limits of Size
A ghost cannot be the size of a mountain because a human consciousness vector does not have the garbon mass to generate a mountain-sized psychosheath. A human psychosheath is calibrated to the scale of a human body. Furthermore, the psychosheath is a field of *resonance*, not kinetic force. It manipulates localized electromagnetic fields (cold spots, flickering lights, moving a light object), but it cannot exert raw physical muscle.

## 3. The Poltergeist Anomaly: The Biological Spill

While ghosts are stuck consciousness vectors, the phenomenon known as the "poltergeist" (noisy ghost) is something entirely different. It is not a consciousness at all.

Classic poltergeist phenomena—flying objects, loud noises, physical disturbances—are overwhelmingly associated with young girls in their early adolescence (ages 12 to 16). 

In SORM, this is a **biological frequency spill**.

When a young girl hits puberty, her biological receiver (the corthexus) is undergoing massive, chaotic rewiring. The brain is building new neural pathways and recalibrating its electromagnetic baseline. 

Because the corthexus filters the psychosheath into physical experience, a massive biological surge in the corthexus causes a corresponding surge in the psychosheath. The psychosheath becomes unstable, over-pressurized, and begins to "leak" or violently discharge localized energy into the surrounding physical environment. The girl's unresolved emotional tension or pubescent chaos acts as the trigger. 

The physical environment gets hit with raw, unstructured kinetic force because the psychosheath is discharging like a static electricity build-up. It is a psychosomatic energetic discharge, not a trapped spirit.
