## What Are These Bridges?

The Ψ-Ontic Reality Model (ΨORM) is a unified framework that repositions consciousness as the primary agent of reality navigation. It is a comprehensive model that draws on physics, neuroscience, philosophy, and the world's spiritual traditions.

However, not everyone comes to ΨORM from the same starting point. A physicist approaches reality differently than a Buddhist, and a Christian approaches it differently than a materialist.

These bridges are **accessible introductions** to ΨORM, tailored to specific worldviews. Each bridge uses the language, concepts, and values of its audience to present ΨORM as a complementary framework — not a replacement.

The goal is not to change anyone's beliefs. It is to invite exploration.
